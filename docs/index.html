About the stay

A brand-new retreat designed around you

Escape to your private retreat in Twynam Heights, East Nassau. This stylish 1-bedroom apartment is perfect for couples, solo travelers, or business trips, comfortably sleeping up to two guests. Enjoy modern comforts like 65-inch Smart TVs, a spa-like rainfall shower, and a quiet residential setting just minutes from the beach.

The space is fully self-contained and thoughtfully designed for comfort, privacy, and convenience on short or extended stays. Airport pickup, in-room dining, room décor, and personalized concierge services are all available to make your stay seamless and unforgettable.

You'll have exclusive, private access to the entire apartment — bedroom, living area, bathroom, and every amenity — with your own private entrance. No indoor spaces are shared with the main house or other guests, ensuring your complete privacy.

The space

Every corner, considered

Bedroom

A comfortable queen-size bed dressed in fresh linens, air conditioning, and ample storage. Guests enjoy a 65-inch Smart TV, an Alexa Show, and cozy robes and slippers waiting in the closet.

Living area

A plush sectional sofa and a second 65-inch Smart TV with Fire Stick and Netflix — perfect for movie nights, sports viewing, or relaxing after a day out. A collection of board and card games, including Jenga, Chess, and UNO, is on hand.

Spa-like bathroom

A luxurious rainfall shower with a built-in sitting bench, ideal for comfort and relaxation. Fresh towels and essential toiletries — shampoo, conditioner, body soap and more — are always provided.

Kitchen

A fully equipped kitchen with stove, oven, refrigerator and freezer, dishwasher, coffee maker, kettle and toaster — plus cookware, dishes and wine glasses. Complimentary snacks and bottled water greet you on arrival.

In-unit laundry

A full-size in-unit washer and dryer makes the apartment especially convenient for longer stays — laundry access is included for stays of three nights or more.
